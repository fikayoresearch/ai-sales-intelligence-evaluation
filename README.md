# AI Sales Intelligence — AI Model Evaluation & B2B Sales Research

**Independent Portfolio Project | November 2026**

A research-led project evaluating how effectively four AI platforms perform B2B lead qualification, independent company research and commercial sales analysis.

Using ten UK hospitality brands and a fictional SaaS platform called **GuestFlow**, I designed two experiments to compare rule-following accuracy against real-world research reliability.

## Project Overview

**Research question:** Can AI reliably identify and prioritise commercial opportunities, and where is human verification still necessary?

The project evaluates four platforms:
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- DeepSeek (DeepThink)

## Key Results

### Experiment A — Rule-Based Lead Qualification

All four platforms correctly applied a predefined scoring rubric across ten companies.

**40/40 scores and 40/40 priority classifications matched the reference dataset.**

This demonstrated reliable rule application within the tested sample, but did not assess independent research ability.

### Experiment B — Independent Commercial Research

Each platform researched the same ten companies and produced commercial recommendations. Responses were assessed across five dimensions: research accuracy, commercial reasoning, strategic awareness, risk recognition and actionable sales intelligence.

| AI Platform | Audited Score |
|---|---:|
| DeepSeek DeepThink | 73/100 |
| Claude | 70/100 |
| ChatGPT | 62/100 |
| Gemini | 31/100 |

**40 material research claims were audited**, revealing factual discrepancies, incomplete citations, overlooked software competitors and unsupported commercial assumptions.

*Limitations: One run per platform, ten companies and a single human-directed, AI-assisted evaluation process. Results describe the tested outputs, not general model performance.*

## GuestFlow — SaaS Financial Simulator

Developed a spreadsheet-based financial model to demonstrate how verified sales intelligence can inform B2B SaaS revenue decisions.

The simulator includes subscription pricing, account-level annualised revenue opportunities, optional analytics, transaction fees, sales pipeline forecasting and downside/base/upside scenarios.

**GuestFlow is fictional.** All financial scenarios are illustrative and do not represent actual customers, contracted revenue or guaranteed sales forecasts.

## Key Takeaways

1. Accurate rule-following does not guarantee accurate independent research.
2. AI-generated commercial recommendations require verification of sources, company relationships and incumbent technology.
3. Reliable sales forecasting requires transparent assumptions and a clear separation between recurring revenue and variable fees.
4. AI is most useful as part of an auditable, human-reviewed research and decision-making workflow.
